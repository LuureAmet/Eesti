## Soul (one line, do not dilute)
Estonian manor houses (mõisad) as living, inspectable culture: every item artifactable — previewable in different restoration choices — so a visitor designs their own ideal version before any transaction exists.

## Betrayal test
- Becoming a real-estate listing site or a static photo gallery — the *interactive restoration choice* IS the site.
- 3D tech (frameworks, WebGL bloat) that breaks the old-device/no-JS rule; the 2.5D SVG approach was chosen precisely to avoid this.

## Standing on
2.5D SVG manor mockup committed (07-19): restoration studio, role lenses, register. The artifact object model was EXTRACTED FROM ramm into varamu + artifact API — ramm is the pattern's birthplace. content.json, skin.css present.

## Next honest step (one thing)
Wire one manor item end-to-end as data: pick a single element from the SVG mockup (a roof, a window) and represent its restoration variants as artifact rows (kind:"variant") with artifact_relation linking them, rendered from content.json statically. One item, full chain, nothing more — proves the artifactable loop before scaling to a whole manor.

## Best-day vision
A catalog where each manor is a small world: walk it in 2.5D, lift any piece, see it under three restoration philosophies, save your version as your artifact, share it as a portal link. Culture + commerce + community meet when real manor communities adopt their page and haaletus runs their restoration votes. The role lenses (owner/restorer/historian/dreamer) become the cluster's showcase of per-viewer visibility done as rule rows.
