## Soul (one line, do not dilute)
Where ideas meet investors and doers — and hand off to seadus/koduteenus the moment something real forms; the site's success is measured by successful *departures* from it.

## Betrayal test
- Idea hoarding or claim-staking mechanics that make people afraid to share.
- Becoming a pitch-theater; the currency is honest idea development, not performance.

## Standing on
index.html + content.json + SITE_SPEC exist ("core concept clear, details open"). Append-only message/artifact tables give ideas an honest timeline for free.

## Next honest step (one thing)
Model the idea lifecycle as data: `{kind:"idee", payload:{seed_text, stage:"seeme|kasvab|kohtub|lahkus", needs:[], history:[]}}` — where "lahkus" (departed) is the SUCCESS state linking to where it went (seadus agreement, koduteenus job, own site). One real example idea in content.json (there is no shortage in this cluster — e.g. the thumbnail machine seed) rendered statically.

## Best-day vision
An idea arrives as a seed with a timestamped, append-only history; helpers and backers attach themselves as visible relations; when it matures it *leaves* — to a seadus agreement, a koduteenus arrangement, or its own portal — and the departure is celebrated on the page. The idea graveyard is equally honest: dormant seeds stay browsable, revivable, attributed. Ideas as commons with provenance.

## Seeds (designed, NOT built)
- Interest/backing as observation rows with identity layers (trigger: first real backer).
- Departure ceremony → krattiina shard (trigger: first real departure).
- Idea-similarity surfacing once pgvector lands (trigger: >200 ideas).

## Guardrails specific to this site
Timestamped provenance protects idea authors — never allow silent edits that could rewrite who-said-what-when. No ownership claims enforced by the site; it records, it does not arbitrate.
